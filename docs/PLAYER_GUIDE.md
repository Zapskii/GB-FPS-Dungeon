# GB-FPS-Dungeon — Player Guide

You wake on the first of four floors of a dungeon. Each floor is a new
random maze. Find the key, open the door, take the stairs down. Clear
floor 4 to escape.

## Controls

| Button | Action |
|---|---|
| D-pad up / down | Walk forward / back |
| D-pad left / right | Turn |
| B + left / right | Strafe (sidestep) |
| A | Shoot (about 4 shots a second, aims at screen centre) |
| Select | Open the automap (the game pauses) |

On the automap:

| Button | Action |
|---|---|
| Select or A | Close the map |
| B | Music on / off |
| Start | Save to a slot |

## The screen

- **Top row of the HUD:** your hearts (up to 5), `K` keys held, `F` current
  floor, a gem and your gold count, and a compass. The letter for the way
  you're facing is highlighted.
- **Bottom row:** build number and frames per second. You can ignore these.

## Items

Items are white. They get bigger as you walk closer. To pick one up,
walk over it.

| | Item | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| <img src="img/key2.png" width="64"> | **Key** | Opens a door. It always lies in the dead end farthest from where you start. Keys don't carry over to the next floor. |
| <img src="img/health2.png" width="64"> | **Heart** | Gives back one heart. If your hearts are full, it stays where it is, so you can come back for it later. Each floor has 2. |
| <img src="img/gold2.png" width="64"> | **Gem** | Adds to your gold. Each floor has 2. Your total shows on the win and game-over screens. |
| <img src="img/map2.png" width="64"> | **Map scroll** | Shows the whole floor on the automap. |
| <img src="img/stairs2.png" width="64"> | **Stairs** (down arrow) | Takes you to the next floor. You can't come back up. On floor 4 they lead outside, and you win. |

**Doors** are the dark grey wall blocks marked **D**. Walk into one while
you have a key to open it. The stairs are always in a small room behind
the door, so you need the key first.

## Enemies

Enemies hunt you through the corridors and hurt you on contact. When you
get hit the screen flashes. When an enemy takes a shot but survives, it
flashes white and stops for a moment.

| | Enemy | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| <img src="img/grunt2.png" width="72"> | **Grunt** | The dark blob. Dies to 1 shot on floors 1–2 and needs 2 shots on floors 3–4. |
| <img src="img/bat2.png" width="72"> | **Bat** | Flies at eye level and moves twice as fast as a grunt, but one shot kills it. Floor *n* has *n* bats. |
| (a bigger grunt) | **Brute** | Slow (half grunt speed) and takes 3 shots. 1 on floor 3, 2 on floor 4. |

Enemies more than about 10 steps away stay still until you come closer.

## Saving and loading

There are 3 save slots, and each save also keeps the floor number.

- **To save:** press Select to open the map, then Start. Choose a slot with
  left / right and press Start or A. B cancels.
- **To load:** once you have a save, the title and GAME OVER screens show
  **A LOAD SAVE**. Press A, choose the slot, and you continue from where you
  saved.

## Winning and losing

- **Losing all your hearts** ends the run. The GAME OVER screen shows the
  floor you reached, your kills and your gold.
- **Taking the stairs on floor 4** wins the game. The win screen shows
  your kills, your time in seconds (time on the map doesn't count) and your
  gold.

Press Start on either screen to begin a new run.

## Tips

- Strafe (B + left/right) to look around a corner without walking into an
  enemy.
- Check the map often. Scrolls show the whole floor, and the map pauses
  the game, so you can plan your route.
- Bats are fast but weak. Turn to face them and shoot.
- On floors 3–4, back away while you shoot brutes. They're too slow to
  catch you.
- If you're at full health, leave the heart. It'll still be there when
  you need it.

## Super Game Boy

On a Super Game Boy (or an emulator set to SGB mode) the game is framed by a
stone-brick border. On a regular Game Boy nothing changes.
